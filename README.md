one image summing up the world news of Wednesday, 18 November 2026.

## The image

`assets/entry.png` — a 1024×1024 editorial infographic, "WORLD NEWS — Wednesday,
18 November 2026", with the day's six top stories plus an in-brief strip:

1. **Gulf crisis** — U.S. strikes Iran's Qeshm Island; Iran fires drones at U.S.
   radar sites in Bahrain and Patriot batteries in Kuwait.
2. **Ukraine** — Kerch Bridge partly collapses under Ukrainian attack.
3. **Summit** — Trump, Xi and Putin to meet in Shenzhen at APEC.
4. **Health** — Ebola outbreak in DR Congo more than doubles: 11,000 cases,
   5,400 deaths.
5. **United States** — America loses measles-free status after 26 years.
6. **Disaster** — Russian military plane crashes in Siberia: 19 dead.

In brief: Democrats win Alaska Senate seat (56 seats); Category 5 Cyclone
Xavier hits the Cook Islands; Gaza cold kills in displaced people's tents;
U.S. flights delayed after a nationwide FAA ground stop.

Sources: Reuters, AP, BBC, Al Jazeera, CBS News, The Japan Times, United
Nations — all reported 18 November 2026.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13) copies `assets/entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
It is a pure byte copy: no network, no randomness, no timestamps — running it
twice writes byte-identical output.

```
python3 main.py
```
